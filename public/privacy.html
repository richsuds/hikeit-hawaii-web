Privacy Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026

HikeIt Hawaii provides trail-planning information without requiring a user account on this website. Our server may receive standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and timestamps for security, reliability, and aggregate usage measurement.

Weather and trail information

Trail searches and filters are processed to provide the requested experience. Condition estimates combine public trail records and third-party weather or stream data. We do not sell trail searches or precise location information.

Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, including pages and trail details viewed, searches, filters, shares, outbound map or permit links, app-download links and social referrals. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or precise trail-user locations to Analytics.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled and you grant permission, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements. Advertising is kept separate from weather warnings, access restrictions, maps, driving directions and trail-condition estimates.

Third-party links

The site links to mapping, permitting, land-management, weather, analytics, advertising and other third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your choices

You can choose “Necessary only” when the privacy prompt appears. You can revisit your choice through the Privacy choices link in the site footer and restrict cookies through your browser settings. For privacy questions, email hikeithawaii@gmail.com.
