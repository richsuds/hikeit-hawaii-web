Advertising and editorial independence

Advertising helps support HikeIt Hawaii’s weather, mapping, and trail-data services. Paid placements are labeled as advertisements or sponsored content.

Safety and access information are not for sale

Advertisers cannot purchase favorable trail-condition estimates, alter weather or water data, influence access notices, or change the order of official warnings. Ads are not placed inside weather-alert banners, maps, driving directions, or access restrictions.

Interested in sponsoring HikeIt Hawaii?

We welcome responsible local partnerships that are useful to hikers and respectful of Hawaiʻi’s land and communities. Sponsorship availability and contact details will be published here when the program opens.
